# Independent Reader Test

Review the document as if you have no access to the author's conversation.

## Comprehension checks
- Can you explain the problem and why it matters?
- Is the intended audience clear?
- Is the requested decision or action explicit?
- Are key terms and acronyms defined?
- Can you distinguish facts from assumptions?
- Are alternatives and trade-offs understandable?
- Are owners, dates, dependencies, and risks explicit?
- Can a reader find the next step without asking the author?

## Findings format
For each issue, report:
- Location.
- Severity: blocker, significant, or minor.
- What a reader is likely to misunderstand.
- A concrete proposed fix.

Do not invent reader feedback. Label simulated reader questions as a desk review, not as a real user test.
